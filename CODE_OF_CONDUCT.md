# Code of conduct

Moviestracker is a small project made by people in their free time. We want
everyone who uses it or works on it to feel welcome, whatever their
experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a
  language that is not their first.
- Assume good intent, and critique ideas and code, never people.
- Accept decisions and feedback gracefully, and give credit where it is due.
- Keep discussions on topic. This project does not help anyone find or share
  content they are not allowed to use; do not ask for it.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, threats,
publishing other people's private information, and sustained disruption, in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[a private advisory](https://github.com/lieranderl/moviestracker-app/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even though it is not a security problem) or by contacting
@lieranderl on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and contributions, and warn, suspend
or ban anyone whose behavior breaks this code, in proportion to what
happened.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
